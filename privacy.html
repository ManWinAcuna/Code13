Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

What we collect

If you join the early-access list, we store the email address you give us.

If you create a Code13 account, we store your account email and the information you enter into the app, such as birth dates, names, and profiles you save. This is the data the app needs to show you your readings and keep them across devices.

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), protected by security rules so that only your own signed-in account can read or write your data.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout pages. We never see or store your card number. Stripe shares with us the email you used to pay, so we can attach your purchase to your Code13 account. Stripe's handling of your payment data is covered by Stripe's privacy policy.

What we do with it

We use your email to contact you about Code13: your early-access signup, your account, and your purchase. We use your saved data only to run the app for you.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with anyone except the services named above that make the app work.

What stays on your device

Most of what the app computes lives in your browser's local storage on your own device. If you never create an account, your entries never leave your device.

Deleting your data

Email us at horseyear2026manuel@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account data and remove you from the early-access list.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
